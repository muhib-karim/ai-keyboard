# AI Keyboard — X/Twitter Launch Thread

## Launch Thread (Twitter/X)

🧵 Introducing AI Keyboard — the first Android keyboard with fully offline AI voice typing powered by user-selectable models! 🚀

1/ 🔒 Privacy-First: Your voice NEVER leaves your device. 100% offline processing. No cloud servers. No data tracking. Your AI. Your models. Your data.

2/ 🤖 Choose Your AI Model: Install any ONNX or Vosk ASR model:
   • NVIDIA Parakeet 0.6B (best accuracy)
   • Distil-Whisper ONNX (fast, English-only)
   • Vosk (lightweight, low-end devices)
   Full control over how your device understands your voice.

3/ ⌨️ Modern Keyboard Features:
   • Smart auto-correct & next-word predictions
   • Swipe/gesture typing
   • Emoji, GIF, stickers
   • Clipboard manager
   • Custom themes & layouts
   • Full dark/light themes

4/ 🎨 Fully Customizable:
   • 5+ theme presets
   • Create custom themes
   • Adjustable keyboard height
   • Key appearance customization
   • Material 3 design

5/ 📦 Open Source: Built with Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Apache-2.0 license. Community-driven. Transparent. Your keyboard, your code.

6/ 🚀 Available Now on Google Play Store! Download AI Keyboard and experience typing reimagined — powered by offline AI.
